## Installation

- Windows 10/11 x64: download the `Setup.exe` file.
- macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon: download the `.dmg` file, then drag BOTW
  Companion to Applications.

Python, offline data, JoyConDSU, and SDL3 are included.
